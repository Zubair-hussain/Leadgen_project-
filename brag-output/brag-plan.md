# Brag Plan: LeadGen

## What is this app?
LeadGen finds the B2B prospects who are already looking for you — pick a niche, an
area, and a channel, and it hands back a short, scored list of buyers instead of a
phone book.

## The angle
Quiet confidence. This isn't a loud "10x your pipeline" pitch — it's a premium
product film that lets the product's own line do the work: **"Stop chasing
strangers. Start winning buyers."** The centerpiece is the real demo engine
running: SerpAPI returns 128, crosses the 100 threshold, pulls in Apify + Google
SerpAPI, and settles on 47 qualified, scored leads. Specific numbers, real copy,
restraint.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. The crimson→brass **LeadGen** wordmark resolves at scale with a soft
gold glow, one quiet line beneath: *"Find the people already looking for you."*

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero line lands: **"Stop chasing strangers."** → **"Start winning buyers."**
  with *winning* shifting to brass (mirrors the site's animated word).
- The engine console types out: `SerpAPI → 128 results`, then
  `128 > 100 · engaging Apify + Google SerpAPI`, then a gold `✓ 47 qualified leads`.
- Two lead rows arrive with scores (96 / 100, 92 / 100) — the product *doing* its
  thing, not describing it.

## Outro / punchline
The site's own promise: **"Three answers in, a working list out."** → the LeadGen
wordmark, *by Zubair Hussain*, a hairline `Next.js · Django`. Fade to black.

## User flow worth showing
The demo happy path (entry → key action → result):
1. Three answers in — niche, area, channel.
2. The engine runs — SerpAPI (128) crosses the threshold and engages Apify +
   Google SerpAPI.
3. Result — 47 qualified leads, each scored, ready to export.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: 4 scenes, long holds, soft crossfades; motion is slow and
  deliberate; typography is light with generous spacing; the numbers carry the
  drama, not effects.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #050505 (near-black; base #000000)
- Accent: brass/gold #c9a84c → #e0b35c, over crimson #c8141e / #ef4444
  (brand gradient: `linear-gradient(110deg,#c8141e,#ef4444 46%,#c9a84c)`)
- Text: #f3ece6 (warm off-white), muted #c9bcb1
- Display font: Cormorant Garamond (serif)
- Body font: DM Sans; Mono: JetBrains Mono (for the console)
- Strongest visual element: the crimson→brass gradient hero with the rotating
  word, and the monospace run console resolving to "47 qualified leads".

## Share copy (draft)
Three answers in, a working list of buyers out — LeadGen finds the people already
looking for you. Built with Next.js + Django. 🔎

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained music bed with sparse, motion-matched accents.
- Music: soft cinematic/corporate bed, low and unobtrusive; gentle fade-in,
  fade to near-silence at the outro.
- Music treatment: start ~0s low; hold under the console; ease down for the final
  line so the tagline lands in near-silence.
- Music cue guidance: track chosen by Hyperframes from bundled assets; target a
  soft swell around the console reveal (~9–14s) and a settle at the outro (~16s).
  Sequential console lines use a slow beat-grid (every other beat) so each line
  holds long enough to read.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the hero glow breathe gently with the bed,
  no waveform bars.
- SFX posture: sparse; a soft key ticks under the typed console lines and one dry,
  low accent on the `✓ 47 qualified leads` and the final wordmark.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed console lines; the count settling on 47; lead rows
  arriving one by one; final logo.
- Restraint rule: no aggressive risers, whooshes, or stingers — this is premium,
  not a hype ad.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Wordmark hook — 4s
Black (#050505). The **LeadGen** wordmark fades/scales in with a crimson→brass
gradient and a soft gold glow. One line settles beneath: *"Find the people already
looking for you."* Long hold.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: a quiet, warm entrance; establish calm confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft low accent as the wordmark settles.
Music: low warm bed fading in.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The line — 5s
Big Cormorant serif. **"Stop chasing strangers."** holds, then **"Start winning
buyers."** with *winning* shifting to brass. Generous spacing, near-black field,
faint red-dot globe texture behind.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the second line arrives after the first holds ~1.5s;
*winning* colour-shifts last.
Audio intent: gentle lift as the promise lands.
Audio-coupled idea: soft swell as "winning" turns brass.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The engine (centerpiece) — 7s
Monospace console on the dark field. Lines type/reveal one by one, each held to
read:
`$ leadgen run --niche "Dental clinics" --area "Austin"`
`→ SerpAPI: 128 results`
`→ 128 > 100 · engaging Apify + Google SerpAPI`
`✓ 47 qualified leads` (gold check).
Then two lead rows slide in: *Northside Group — 96 / 100*, *Harbour Partners —
92 / 100*.
Sequential/interaction: yes — console lines reveal on a slow beat-grid (~0.7–0.9s
apart, each held); the two lead rows arrive one by one after the check.
Audio intent: focused, purposeful; the numbers are the drama.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key ticks under typed lines; a dry low accent on the ✓;
soft card sounds as each lead row arrives.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
Center line: **"Three answers in, a working list out."** holds, then resolves to
the **LeadGen** wordmark, *by Zubair Hussain*, and a hairline `Next.js · Django`.
Ease to black.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: settle to near-silence so the tagline and name land clean.
Audio-coupled idea: one final soft low accent on the wordmark, then fade.
Transition mood: slow crossfade to black → end
