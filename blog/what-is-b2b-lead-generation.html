What Is B2B Lead Generation? A Practical Guide

By Zubair Hussain · 2026-09-16

B2B lead generation is the process of finding businesses that are likely to buy what you sell, collecting a way to reach them, and prioritising the best fits. Done well, it hands your sales effort a short list instead of a phone book.

Where leads come from

  • Search engines and maps (business listings, reviews, websites)
  • Directories and niche marketplaces
  • Social and community signals (hiring, complaints, questions)
  • Job boards and freelance platforms for project-based work

Qualification

Not every match is a lead. Qualification filters on fit (industry, size, location) and intent (are they actively looking or showing a buying signal?).

Scoring

A score ranks leads by fit, signal freshness, and reachability so you contact the strongest first. LeadGen assigns a 0–100 score per lead.

Verify before you send

Sending to dead inboxes hurts deliverability. Verify email syntax and domain (MX) first — see the email deliverability guide below.
