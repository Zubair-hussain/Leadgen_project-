# Design Brief

## Goal

Create a refined, credible lead-generation product experience that feels like a
serious B2B tool while preserving the expressive portfolio-quality landing page.

## Design Principles

- Clear over clever in product screens.
- Smooth motion on the landing page.
- Dense but readable app UI.
- Trustworthy copy with no unsupported claims.
- Dark-first visual identity with warm accent colors.
- Accessible controls and reduced-motion respect.

## Audiences

- Buyer: evaluates the tool as a lead-generation solution.
- User: signs in and manages leads.
- Developer: maintains and extends the project.
- Reviewer: assesses portfolio quality.
